He was always open to answering any questions the class had during lectures, and stayed after class answering them as well. I appreciated how he wrote everything down on the board (and always checked with us before erasing something and moving onto the next topic!). Something Professor Pedro does that helps me understand the material is not just go through steps and motions of solving problems, but allocating time to helping the class understand the intuition behind concepts, which is my favorite, and what I believe is the most effective, form of learning.”

I found that the speed at which Pedro went through topics was good a fostered a deeper understanding of each concept.”
