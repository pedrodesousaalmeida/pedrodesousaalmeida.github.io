Research

I study how changes in the conditions firms face affect prices, service quality, and market structure. I use the airline industry as a setting.

Job Market Paper
The Downstream Effects of Airport Privatization: Evidence from Brazil

I examine how the privatization of airports in Brazil affects airline operations and the markets those airports serve. Privatization improves services provided to both airlines and passengers. Delays decline on the routes served by privatized airports, and passengers report greater satisfaction with those airports. In contrast, market concentration and average fares rise. Airlines that already have more pronounced operations at a privatized airport gain market share on the routes the airport serves, while airlines with little presence lose share. Effects are stronger after the deadline for the mandated infrastructure investments.

Other Working Papers
Reduced Capacity and Price Dispersion: Evidence from a Regional Shock in Brazilian Air Travel

I examine how shocks to airline network capacity affect price dispersion. I use a plausibly exogenous reduction in flights to and from the Brazilian state of Rondônia following increased litigation risk from steep fines for delays and cancellations. Price dispersion declines after the persistent capacity reduction and gradually returns to previous levels.
